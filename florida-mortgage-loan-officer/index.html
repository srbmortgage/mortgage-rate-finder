Florida home purchase loan and home equity options

Homeowners considering Florida home purchase loan should review more than the advertised rate. Loan programs available in Florida

Common programs include home purchase loans, mortgage refinance, HELOCs, FHA loans, conventional loans, VA loans, jumbo loans, investment property loans. Conventional loans may work well for borrowers with established credit and flexible down payment options. FHA loans may help borrowers who need more flexible credit or down payment guidelines.
